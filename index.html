An Open Framework & Taxonomy

Six layers. One map of the data center ecosystem.

From concrete to compute, The Data Center Stack maps the functions that make up the industry, not the companies inside it. Free to use, adapt, and cite under CC BY 4.0.

1
Foundation
Land, power procurement, water rights, permitting, entitlement
2
Infrastructure
Building shell, electrical & cooling systems, connectivity backbone
3
Deployment
Rack & stack, structured cabling, systems integration, migration & relocation
4
Operations
Facility management, monitoring, security, maintenance
5
Platform
Virtualization, orchestration, managed infrastructure services
6
Workloads
The applications and business logic running on top
